# Orbit

**Modern, open-source project management platform built for developers, teams, and organizations.**

Orbit is a modern project management platform focused on simplicity, performance, and complete control over your data. It combines task management, documentation, planning, and collaboration into a single cohesive workspace while remaining fully self-hostable and open source.

Our goal is to build a platform that developers genuinely enjoy using—without unnecessary complexity, vendor lock-in, or hidden limitations.

---

## Vision

Project management software should be fast, intuitive, extensible, and transparent.

Orbit is being built around the idea that every team, regardless of its size, should have access to a professional project management platform that can be hosted anywhere and customized to fit its workflow.
